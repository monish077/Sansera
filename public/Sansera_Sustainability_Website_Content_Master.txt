PURPOSE

This document consolidates website-ready sustainability and ESG content
from the supplied Sansera Engineering Sustainability Report FY2023-24
and Annual Report FY2024-25.

IMPORTANT REPORTING NOTE

The reports cover different periods. Use FY2024-25 Annual Report figures
as the latest/current figures where available. Use FY2023-24
Sustainability Report figures for detailed historical initiatives and
programme descriptions. Always display the reporting year next to a KPI.
Do not combine figures from different years into one metric.

=============================================================== 1. CORE
SUSTAINABILITY POSITIONING
===============================================================

Sansera Engineering views sustainability as part of responsible growth
and precision manufacturing. Its ESG approach focuses on reducing
environmental impact, increasing renewable-energy use, improving
resource efficiency, protecting employee health and safety, supporting
communities, strengthening responsible sourcing and maintaining strong
governance.

Recommended hero headlines: - Engineering a Cleaner Future. - High
Precision for a Sustainable Future. - Precision Engineering. Responsible
Progress. - Clean Energy. Smarter Manufacturing. - Engineering Progress

while reducing environmental impact, protecting people, strengthening

===============================================================

including Solar, Wind and GC Hybrid. - Clean energy accounted for 48%

wind was reported.

rooftop solar at Plant 9. - Planned 1,000 kW installation at Plant 11. -
Planned 7,000 MWh/year solar power supply for Plant 5. - 27,000 MWh/year
hybrid power supply for the Karnataka plant was in progress.

Targets: - Increase renewable-energy share to 80% by FY2030. - Rooftop
solar at feasible plants by 2026.

=============================================================== 5.
ENERGY MANAGEMENT AND EFFICIENCY
===============================================================

FY2023-24 energy data: Total energy consumption: - FY2022-23: 141,311
MWh - FY2023-24: 164,975 MWh

Energy intensity: - FY2022-23: 6.76 MWh / Rs. million turnover -
FY2023-24: 6.47 MWh / Rs. million turnover

Energy-efficiency initiatives: 1. Regular energy audits. 2. Auto-cutoff
mechanisms during machinery idle time. 3. Heat pumps replacing electric
heaters in washing machines. 4. Priority procurement of IE3/IE4 motors.
5. IE3/IE4 motor efficiency of approximately 93–95%. 6. Aluminium
transient pipes for compressed-air distribution. 7. Energy-efficient air
screw compressors. 8. Energy efficiency considered in inbuilt SPM
design. 9. Pump/motor optimization in deep-hole drilling machines. 10.
LPG-to-PNG transition at multiple plants. 11. Photosensors to optimize
lighting. 12. Waste-heat recovery using compressors in washing
processes. 13. Continuous improvement through the Energy Management
System.

Energy Policy: Sansera’s Energy Policy is aligned with ISO 50001:2018
and focuses on energy performance, efficient products/services, resource
optimization, employee training, targets and regulatory compliance.

The Annual Report 2024-25 states that all Sansera plants are certified
under ISO 50001:2018 for Energy Management Systems.

Website section: “Making Every Unit of Energy Count”

Copy: “Sansera combines renewable-energy procurement with operational
efficiency. Energy audits, intelligent machine controls, efficient
motors, optimized compressed-air systems, heat recovery and smarter
lighting help reduce avoidable energy use across manufacturing
operations.”

===============================================================

brick-sized blocks for third-party recycling.

scrap recycling, material recovery and waste diversion.

FY2023-24 Sustainability Report.

such as cement.

Targets: - Annual Report 2024-25: zero waste to landfill across

=============================================================== 21.
MATERIAL ESG RISKS AND OPPORTUNITIES
===============================================================

GHG emissions: Risk from emissions-intensive manufacturing and
increasing customer, investor and regulatory expectations. Mitigation:
energy audits, renewable energy, energy efficiency and lower-
energy-footprint equipment. Opportunity: lower costs, stronger ESG
positioning and customer preference.

Waste: Risk from improper hazardous-waste handling. Opportunity:
circular economy, scrap recycling and zero-landfill practices.

Energy: Opportunity to reduce cost, improve efficiency and support
emissions reduction. All plants are certified under ISO 50001:2018
according to the FY2024-25 Annual Report.

Product responsibility: The shift toward EVs increases the importance of
product innovation. Greater EV-product development can strengthen
customer relationships and future readiness.

Occupational safety: Risks include physical hazards, chemical exposure,
vehicle movement, ergonomic strain, respiratory risks, noise and fire.
Mitigation includes integrated EHSQ, HIRA, training, ISO 45001 and
medical support.

DEI: Opportunity to improve inclusion, engagement, creativity,
innovation and employer reputation.

Supply chain: Risks include irresponsible sourcing, labour/human-rights
violations, child labour, unsafe workplaces and environmental
non-compliance. Mitigation includes responsible sourcing, sustainable
procurement, supplier assessments and conflict-mineral controls.
